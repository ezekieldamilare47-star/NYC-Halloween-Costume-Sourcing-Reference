## Buy vs. Rent vs. Alter

| Option | Best for | Trade-offs | Reference store support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buy | Costumes you will reuse, makeup, consumables, kids sizes that get worn hard | Higher upfront cost, storage needed | Full retail floor plus same-day local delivery and free local pickup |
| Rent | One-night events, elaborate or theatrical costumes, prop-heavy looks | Return deadline, security deposit | Costume rentals; Time Out New York noted three-day rentals start at $100 with a security deposit. Prop rentals also available |
| Alter | Fit problems, combining pieces, upgrading an off-the-rack costume | Lead time, especially in October | Costume alterations offered in-store |

Rule of thumb: buy anything that touches skin (makeup, prosthetics, contacts, fake teeth), rent anything elaborate you will wear once, and alter anything that almost works.

## Costume Planning Timeline

| Weeks before event | Task |
|---|---|
| 8+ | Choose a character or theme; confirm group or couples concept |
| 6 | Visit or browse the store; shortlist costume, wig and mask options |
| 5 | Decide buy vs. rent vs. alter; reserve rentals early |
| 4 | Book alterations; buy makeup and SFX supplies |
| 3 | Run a full makeup test, including adhesive and removal |
| 2 | Book pro makeup application if needed; pick up accessories |
| 1 | Final fitting; confirm rental pickup and return dates |
| 0 | Event day: allow extra time for makeup and travel |

October is the busiest month at the reference store, so anything requiring staff time (rentals, alterations, makeup appointments) should be locked in early.

## SFX Makeup Checklist

- [ ] Base face and body makeup in the right tones
- [ ] Special FX kit matched to the look (wounds, aging, creature)
- [ ] Prosthetic pieces plus compatible adhesive
- [ ] Adhesive remover and gentle cleanser
- [ ] Fake blood (consider a non-staining formula for fabric)
- [ ] Costume contacts, purchased with proper eye-safety guidance
- [ ] Fake teeth or fangs, test-fitted before the event
- [ ] Setting spray or powder for long nights
- [ ] Brushes, sponges and stipple tools
- [ ] Patch test on skin at least 48 hours before the event
- [ ] Optional: pro makeup application booking
